What is Takees Marketing?

Takees Marketing is a full-service digital marketing agency based in Kannur, Kerala, with an office in UB City, Bangalore. We help businesses grow online through SEO, social media marketing, website design, branding, paid advertising, and video production. We serve startups, small businesses, and growing brands across Kerala, Bangalore, and India.

HOW WE STARTED

Our Story

Kannur Headquarters
Takees Park, Airport Road, Mele Chovva, Kannur, Kerala 670006
Bangalore Office
UB City, Level 15, Concord Tower 1, Vittal Mallya Road, Bangalore 560001

Takees Marketing started with a simple belief: Indian businesses deserve digital marketing that actually delivers real revenue, not vanity metrics and empty promises.

Founded by Akhinil Thabraiz in Kannur, our journey began by helping local enterprises, clinics, builders, and educational institutions build a credible digital presence through disciplined execution and measurable customer acquisition.

As our clients grew, so did our footprint. Today, operating from our headquarters at Takees Park in Kannur and our strategic hub at UB City Bangalore, we manage end-to-end digital growth for ambitious brands across India and the GCC.

“There is an enormous gap between what agencies promise and what they deliver. We started Takees to fix that — bringing strategy, engineering, and creative excellence into a single focused team.”

From early-stage ventures seeking their first 1,000 customers to established multi-crore enterprises scaling pan-India, our team operates not as an outside vendor, but as an embedded growth partner accountable for business results.
