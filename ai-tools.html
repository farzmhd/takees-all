GEMINI PROMPT TEXT

In the middle of the night, a man dressed in a warm padded jacket is standing in the middle of the street in Times Square, New York City. The streets are busy, with glowing neon light effects and yellow taxi cabs passing by. He is standing under a streetlight with warm light shining on him. The photo is taken from eye level, with a 35mm lens, capturing the vibrant atmosphere of Times Square at night with glowing lights and vivid colors, giving an energetic feel to the photo.

He has short dark, slightly curly hair, dark brown eyes, a soft expression with a faint, warm smile. The street light casts a soft light on his face, highlighting his sharp jawline and the texture of his jacket, while the background is filled with blurred neon lights and passing cars, adding depth and dynamic motion to the scene.

His posture is relaxed, yet present as he stands slightly off-center, with his hands tucked into his jacket pockets. The atmosphere is quiet despite the bustling city around him, creating an intimate moment amidst the noise of Times Square.

The contrast between the calm figure and the energetic city highlights the quiet strength of the subject. The reflection of neon lights on the wet pavement adds a cinematic quality to the composition, while the soft light on his face accentuates his features, making him the focus of the photo despite the busy background.

Camera & Style Details:
Shot on 35mm monochrome film (ISO 400), sharp subject focus, shallow depth of field, high contrast lighting, urban noir photography style, realistic grain & specular highlights.
